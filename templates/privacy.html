Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service to you. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and personalizing your experience. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. You are responsible for keeping your account password confidential.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your personal data and expense records will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use essential infrastructure services (such as hosting providers) to operate the app, but these providers are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks or analytics platforms that track you across the web.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies only to keep you logged in while you use the app. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party cookies.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly.
